# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Positive behavior includes empathy, constructive feedback, respect for differing viewpoints, and responsibility for mistakes. Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, sexualized attention, insults, intimidation, publication of private information, or conduct that would reasonably be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the project maintainer using the contact method on the GitHub profile. Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently ban participants whose behavior is inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.
